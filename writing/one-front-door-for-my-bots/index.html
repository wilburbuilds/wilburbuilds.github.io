One front door for my bots

October 20, 2026

I started with one assistant. Then I wanted one for training, one for reading, one for markets, one for the house. Before long I had a dozen, and every one of them wanted to talk to me.

The fix was boring: one front door. A single main assistant takes my requests and hands each one to the bot that owns it. The specialists do their work and only come back when something needs me, like a decision, a deadline, or a problem. When nothing changed, they stay quiet.

Two rules made it work. Every bot has one clear job and no overlap. And nothing goes out under my name without me pressing send.

It's less about AI and more about operations: clear owners, clean handoffs, and silence when nothing needs me.
