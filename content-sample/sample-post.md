**This is a sample post.** It lives in the repo under `content-sample/` and is only used when the Obsidian `writing/` folder has no notes marked `publish: true`. Once you publish a real note, this one disappears from the site.

## What a post can use

Regular Markdown works: *emphasis*, **bold**, ==highlights==, [external links](https://github.com/wilburbuilds), lists, quotes and code.

- A link to another published note, like [[Sample post]], becomes a real link.
- A link to a note that isn't published, like [[Some private draft]], becomes plain text.
- Embeds such as private images or notes are dropped.
